Selected Projects

KIH: Keyboard In my Hand team of 4 · team lead · Grand Prize

A glove-type wearable Hangul keyboard for eyes-free Korean text entry. 16 tactile switches are embedded across the phalanxes of a pair of gloves (8 per hand) and pressed by the thumbs. Input is sequential rather than chorded; repeated taps on the same button add strokes, mirroring the graphical derivation principle (가획) of Hangul. Enumerates as a plug-and-play USB HID keyboard — no drivers or companion app required.

Motivated by three gaps: (a) VR/AR text entry where physical keyboards break down, (b) affordable tactile input for Korean visually-impaired users, and (c) posture-related VDT syndrome (7.05 M reported cases in Korea, 2024).

Results: KSPC 1.27 (26% over frequency-blind layouts), 9–15 WPM in pilot use, per-user timing calibration stored in EEPROM, total BOM ₩14,184. Stack: Arduino Leonardo (ATmega32u4), native USB HID. Recognition: Grand Prize, SNU Mechatronics Competition (Dec 2025); exhibited at the 15th SNU College of Engineering Creative Design Festival (2026).
